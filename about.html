About This Project

This visual novel was created as the final project for the GEWORLD subject, Group 5. It follows two perspectives inside the same workplace, telling a difficult story about harassment and the choices, and lack of thereof, that shape it.

Declaration of AI Usage

No AI was used in writing the story. The dialogue, choices, and endings are entirely the team's own work. AI tools were used only for the following, unrelated to the writing:

Gemini

Claude Code
